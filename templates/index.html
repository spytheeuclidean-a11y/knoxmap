                Drawing board

                Click a tool, then click on the sheet to draw. Double-click to finish a line or a shape. Click a thing to pick it: Delete removes it, the arrow keys nudge it, Escape lets go. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.

                Join a map you have built

                Its streets are marked on the edge they share with this sheet. Draw a road to one and it snaps onto it; the map is put beside it in the world when it is built.

                Building editor

                Pick a room, then drag across the plan to draw it. Drawing over a room takes that much of it away; the eraser cuts the shape of the building. To change a room you have already drawn, take the adjust tool and drag its edge; a room you have taken hold of can then be moved with the arrow keys, copied with Ctrl+D or removed with Delete. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes.
