On adds the buildings OpenStreetMap has not got, from Microsoft’s machine-detected building footprints, same licence. Downloads about 30 MB per tile on the first run, nothing to install. Worth it where your town is half missing from OSM; elsewhere it adds sheds. Falls back to Overture (needs DuckDB) if Microsoft cannot be reached. = Activado añade los edificios que no están en OpenStreetMap a partir de huellas detectadas por IA de Microsoft (misma licencia). Descarga aprox. 30 MB por casilla en la primera ejecución, nada que instalar. Vale la pena donde a tu pueblo le falta la mitad en OSM; en otros sitios añade cobertizos. Usa Overture como alternativa (requiere DuckDB) si Microsoft no responde.
On builds every address as mapped. Off keeps the real roads, rivers, woods and terrain, but leaves out half the houses and grows the rest into proper homes with yards. Named places are always built, at the game’s size. = Activado construye cada dirección tal como está mapeada. Desactivado conserva las carreteras, ríos, bosques y terreno reales, pero omite la mitad de las casas y expande el resto en hogares con patios jugables. Los lugares con nombre siempre se construyen, al tamaño del juego.
On builds the buildings missing from a street that has buildings either side of the gap: the shop missing from a row of shops, the houses from a run of houses. They are made like their neighbours, same width, depth, set-back and storeys, and only where there is room: never across a road, on water, in a park or a wood. Uses nothing but the map itself, so it costs no download. Off leaves the gaps as OpenStreetMap has them. = Activado construye los edificios que faltan en un tramo de calle que tiene edificios a ambos lados del hueco: la tienda que falta en una hilera comercial, las casas de una cuadra residencial. Se crean como sus vecinos (mismo ancho, profundidad, retiro y pisos), y solo donde hay espacio: nunca sobre carreteras, agua, parques o bosques. Usa solo el propio mapa, por lo que no cuesta descarga. Desactivado deja los huecos tal como están en OpenStreetMap.
On builds with the game’s own art only, leaving Erika’s Tiles out even when it is installed. A map built with the mod needs the mod to look right, so this is the one to turn on before handing a map to somebody who has not subscribed. = Activado construye solo con texturas del juego base, excluyendo Erika’s Tiles incluso si está instalado. Un mapa construido con el mod necesita el mod para verse bien, por lo que conviene activar esto antes de compartir el mapa con alguien que no esté suscrito al mod.
On includes roads marked as proposed in OpenStreetMap, even though they may not exist yet. Off leaves them out. = Activado incluye vías marcadas como propuestas en OpenStreetMap, aunque aún no existan. Desactivado las excluye.
On leaves one military rifle on the map: in an army building if there is one, else the police station, else a gun shop, else a house on the edge of town. On plans each house of up to four storeys as a whole: a hall from the front door, the stairs against a wall, the kitchen at the back, bedrooms and the bathroom upstairs, every room with its own window. Anything else is built as before. Needs OR-Tools (pip install ortools).
