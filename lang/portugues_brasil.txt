On adds the buildings OpenStreetMap has not got, from Microsoft’s machine-detected building footprints, same licence. Downloads about 30 MB per tile on the first run, nothing to install. Falls back to Overture (needs DuckDB) if Microsoft cannot be reached. = Ligado adiciona as construções que o OpenStreetMap não tem, das plantas detectadas por máquina da Microsoft, mesma licença. Baixa uns 30 MB por bloco na primeira vez, nada para instalar. Vale a pena onde metade da sua cidade falta no OSM; em outros lugares adiciona galpões. Usa o Overture (precisa do DuckDB) se a Microsoft não responder.
On builds the buildings missing from a street that has buildings either side of the gap: the shop missing from a row of shops, the houses from a run of houses. They are made like their neighbours, same width, depth, set-back and storeys, and only where there is room: never across a road, on water, in a park or a wood.
